This was my first time at FOSSGIS and also my first time attending a conference entirely in German 🇩🇪. Having lived in Germany for over six years now, it was great to finally experience a conference in the native tongue of my chosen country of residence 🥲. To keep the post organized, I'll be talking about the talks I had a chance to attend organized by each day. I'll also mention the talks I didn't see that day that I want to watch later online. I wasn't able to attend the first day, so this post begins on Thursday (March 26th).
